Data Deletion

How to ask Ènvoque Property to delete your personal information.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

You can ask Ènvoque Property to delete the personal information we hold about you at any time, including information we received through Facebook, Instagram or other Meta products.

How to request deletion

Email: marketing@envoqueproperty.com
Subject line: Data deletion request
Phone: (02) 4972 1066 (we will ask you to confirm the request by email)
Post: Privacy Officer, Ènvoque Property, 5/571 Pacific Highway, Belmont NSW 2280
Include: your full name, the email address or phone number you used with us, and (if relevant) the Facebook or Instagram account name you contacted us from.

What happens next

StepWhat we do
1. ConfirmWe reply within 5 business days to confirm it is you, so nobody else can delete your information.
2. DeleteWe delete or de-identify your personal information from our customer records, email lists and marketing systems within 30 days.
3. Confirm againWe email you when deletion is complete, with a reference number for your request.

What we delete

  • Your name, contact details and enquiry notes from our customer records system.
  • Lead form answers, messages and comments we received through Facebook, Instagram or Messenger.
  • Your details from our email and SMS marketing lists.
  • Your details from any advertising audience lists we have uploaded to Meta.

Deleting your information does not remove comments you posted publicly on our Facebook Page or Instagram account from Meta's systems. You can delete those yourself, or ask us to remove them from our Page.

What we may need to keep

Where the law requires it (for example, records of a transaction, tenancy or finance application), we must keep some information for a set period. We will tell you what we are keeping and why, and we delete it when the requirement ends.

Removing Ènvoque from your Facebook or Instagram account

If you connected Ènvoque through Facebook or Instagram, you can also remove our access yourself:

  • On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations).
  • Find Ènvoque and choose Remove.
  • On Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, find Ènvoque and choose Remove.
  • Email us at the address above if you also want the information we already received deleted.

For more about how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.
