  • Enquiry details: what you ask us about, such as an appraisal, a rental, a purchase, a sale, finance or development, and notes from conversations with our team.
  • Property and financial context you choose to share, such as the property you own or want to buy, and your timing.
  • Lead form information you submit through Facebook or Instagram lead ads, forms on our website, or messages sent to our pages.
  • Messages and comments you send to our Facebook Page or Instagram account.
  • Technical and usage data such as device type, pages viewed, and ad interactions, collected through cookies, the Meta Pixel and similar tools.

Applications and services. If you go on to apply for a tenancy, list or buy a property, or apply for finance or insurance through us, we collect the further information that service needs, such as identity documents, employment and income details, rental history and references. We collect this directly from you, through our application and agency systems, and not through Facebook or Instagram. Where the law requires it (for example, anti-money laundering identity checks), we must verify your identity before we can act for you.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information (such as health information, racial or ethnic origin, or political opinions) through social media, ads or website forms, and ask you not to send it to us that way. If a service genuinely needs it, we only collect it with your consent.

You can deal with us without giving your name for general enquiries, but we will need your details to provide most of our services.

4. Facebook, Instagram and Meta platforms

We use Meta products (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Meta advertising tools) to promote our services and to receive enquiries. When you interact with our pages or ads:

  • Information you submit through a Meta lead form is received by us and stored in our customer records system so we can contact you about your enquiry.
  • We access only the data our authorised Meta app permissions allow, such as lead form responses, comments and messages on our own Page and Instagram account, and performance statistics for our own ads.
  • We may send limited, hashed event information (for example, that an enquiry became an appraisal) back to Meta so our advertising can be measured and improved. We do not send your name or contact details in readable form.
  • Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. See facebook.com/privacy/policy and manage your ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use Meta platform data for any purpose other than the ones described in this policy.

Our website and ads may use cookies, the Meta Pixel and similar technologies to understand how people use our site and to measure our advertising. You can control cookies in your browser settings and manage ad tracking in your Meta account settings.

8. Security and retention

We protect personal information with access controls, secure systems and staff training. We keep it only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as the law requires, and then securely delete or de-identify it.

Some records must be kept for set periods under Australian law, for example agency and trust account records under the Property and Stock Agents Act 2002 (NSW), tax records, and identity verification records under anti-money laundering law. Enquiry and marketing records that are not part of a service we provided are deleted when they are no longer needed, or when you ask us to delete them.

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

You can ask us to give you access to the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To delete your data, including information received through Facebook or Instagram, follow the steps on our Data Deletion page. We respond within 30 days.
